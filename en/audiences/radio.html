Random Jingle for Radio Hosts

Use case · ~3 min read

Whether it's local radio, a campus station, or a web stream, a tightly timed show lives on recurring sound elements. Random Jingle plays idents, news jingles, and inserts live on air, with no heavyweight broadcast software required.

Typical moments

  • On the hour: A fixed ident reliably marks every segment start and gives the show structure.
  • News block: A news jingle frames the headlines at the start and end.
  • Ad block: The station ident reliably bridges into and out of the ads.
  • Listener questions or giveaway: A sound bed accompanies live calls in the studio.
  • Weather or traffic: A fixed jingle ritual gives every segment recognizability.
  • Sudden studio hiccup: A filler bridges the seconds until everything's back on track.

Why radio hosts use Random Jingle

Instead of a costly, complex broadcast suite, a browser or installed app is enough: every jingle sits categorized and mapped to a hotkey, so you find the right sound instantly even under live pressure — great for small teams or a campus radio studio.
