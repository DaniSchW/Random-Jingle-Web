Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

Random Jingle is completely free to use. No hidden costs, no pro version.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully offline and locally without signing in. A free account is only useful if you want to sync your board across devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, Random Jingle works entirely without an internet connection.

Which file formats are supported?

MP3 and WAV files are currently supported.
